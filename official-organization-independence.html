02 · Vision and mission

About Miss Elite Universe

Miss Elite Universe is an international beauty pageant initiative founded to celebrate confidence, culture, character, leadership and purpose.

Guided by the motto “Beyond Beauty · Beyond Boundaries · Be The Elite,” the initiative seeks to unite women from different nations and cultural backgrounds through personal growth, cultural exchange and positive representation.

03 · Leadership

Founder & Organization

Miss Elite Universe was founded by Ivan Li, who leads the initiative's vision and development.

The organization continues to develop its international operations, national representation network, partnerships and plans for Miss Elite Universe 2027 in Thailand. Formal authority to represent a registered legal entity must be established by the applicable records and signed authorizations.

04 · Integrity

Organizational Independence

Miss Elite Universe presents its own pageant identity, vision, branding and organizational plans. Its name and activities do not, by themselves, imply any affiliation with another pageant organization.

Formal partnerships, franchises, licenses and affiliations should be identified only according to their actual status and written agreements.
